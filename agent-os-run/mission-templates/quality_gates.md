# quality_gates.md for [Company]'s BoS OS

Checklists the BoS OS runs before a document or message reaches you. The BoS OS names the gate it ran and any item it could not check. When you correct a draft, it proposes one new line here for your yes or no.

---

## Message to a person outside the company

- A named person is the sender.
- No promise or commitment you have not approved.
- Every fact and figure traces to a named source.
- Names and titles are spelled as the person spells them.

## Document with figures or quotes

- Every figure traces to a source the reader can open.
- Every quote is checked word for word against the source.
- Dates and units are stated.

## Claim about the product or the market

- The claim has evidence on file.
- Anything that is an estimate is labelled as one.
